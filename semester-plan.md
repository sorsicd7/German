# Plan: German (self-study, A2 → B1)

*Version 1 · 2026-10-11. Topics: `syllabus.md`. All courses together: `master-plan.md` in this repo (synced copy), or
the online overview page (link in `README.md`).*

**Week numbers** are the same as in the four university courses: **W1 = week of Mon 12 Oct 2026**, H1/H2 = the two
Christmas weeks, W16 = 8–14 Feb 2027 (exam week). Note folders: `weekNN-topic/`.

---

## 0. The plan in one minute

- **One grammar topic per week**, learned properly: explained in simple English, many examples, then exercises one at a time.
- **~4.5 h per week:** Wednesday block (learn the topic) + Friday block (practice, writing, listening) + 15 min
  vocabulary every day + 10 min in the Saturday quiz.
- German never takes time from the university courses. In exam weeks (W15–W16) it drops to vocabulary only.
- **Target: B1 exam in May–July 2027.**

## 3. Targets and red flags
- Weekly German quiz ≥ 80%; the A2 review test (W10) ≥ 80%.

- If a university course is behind, the Friday German block may be used as FLEX **once a month at most**; the Wednesday
  block stays.

# Plan: German (self-study, A2 → B1)

*Version 2 · 2026-10-19 · fresh start on Tue 20 Oct. Topics: `syllabus.md`. All courses together: `master-plan.md` in
this repo (synced copy), or the online overview page (link in `README.md`).*

**Week numbers** are the same in every repo: **W1 = week of Mon 19 Oct 2026** (the plan starts Tue 20 Oct),
H1/H2 = the two Christmas weeks, W15 = 8–14 Feb 2027 (Big Data exam). Note folders: `weekNN-topic/`.

---

## 0. The plan in one minute

- **One grammar topic per week**, learned properly: explained in simple English, many examples, then exercises one at a time.
- **About 5.5 h per week:** Tuesday block (learn the topic) + Friday block (practice and writing) + Wednesday 45 min
  listening + 15 min vocabulary every day + 15 min in the Saturday quiz.
- German never takes time from the three courses. In the Big Data exam week (W15) it drops to vocabulary only.
- **Target: B1 exam (telc or Goethe) in May–July 2027**; book it in February.
